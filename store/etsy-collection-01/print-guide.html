How to print your art

Thank you for your purchase! Your files are true 300 DPI JPGs — they'll print crisp at every size listed below. Three ways to get them on your wall, easiest first:

Option 1 — Online print service (easiest, best value)

  1. Upload the JPG to any poster/photo print service (search "poster printing" — most photo labs, pharmacy photo counters, and online poster shops work).
  2. Choose a size from the matching ratio folder (chart below). Pick matte or lustre paper for this collection's soft, textured look.
  3. If asked, choose "shrink/fit to page" OFF and "crop to fill" ON — the file is already exactly the right proportions.

Option 2 — Local print shop

Bring the JPG on a USB stick or email it. Ask for: "300 DPI poster print, [size], matte paper." Print shops can also print A1/24×36" statement sizes and mount on foam board.

Option 3 — Home printer

Print the 4:5 or ISO-A file on A4/letter cardstock (matte, 200 gsm+ if your printer accepts it). Set print quality to "high/photo," and margins to borderless if supported.

Frame + mat tip: these designs breathe best with a white or off-white mat in a thin black, oak, or walnut frame. Buying frames first? 16×20" frame with an 11×14" mat opening is the classic gallery look — print the 11×14 file.

Hanging your set

  • Trio in a row: same frame size, 2–3" gaps, centers at 57–60" from the floor.
  • Gallery six: 2 rows × 3, or a loose organic cluster — keep gaps consistent (2–3") and the overall shape roughly rectangular.
  • Above bed/sofa: art should span roughly ⅔ the width of the furniture below it; leave 8–10" between headboard and frame bottom.

Troubleshooting

  • Print looks soft? Confirm the shop didn't downscale — the files are 300 DPI at the largest size in each folder; no service should ever ask for "higher resolution."
  • Colors slightly different than screen? Normal — screens are backlit, paper isn't. Matte paper + "no auto-enhance" gives the truest result.
  • Need a size not listed? Message me through Etsy — custom ratios are usually a same-day add.
