# Faceless & touchless income — full deep dive (researched July 2026)

Goal: consistent **$2,000/month** within 6–12 months, no physical products, no shipping,
no face on camera. Method: 107-agent deep-research workflow (3-vote adversarial
verification; 18 claims confirmed, 6 guru statistics refuted) plus three targeted
primary-source agents (content platforms, dev marketplaces, everything else).
~160 agents total across the run.

## The three verified base rates everything hangs on

1. **>54% of indie software products earn $0; ~5% exceed $100k/yr** — the only
   Stripe-VERIFIED revenue dataset found (ScrapingFish scrape of 937 Indie Hackers
   products). Among products that DO reach $1k MRR, median time is ~8 months.
2. **Median one-year-old Etsy shop: 277 sales** (SalesDoe, 797-shop sample) — at digital
   price points that's ~$1–2k gross for year one. The verified winners moved upmarket
   ($20+ products) and stacked catalogs.
3. **Faceless content platforms turned hostile in 2026** (all primary/press-verified):
   YouTube's "inauthentic content" enforcement terminated 16 faceless channels
   (~35M subs, ~$10M/yr) in Jan 2026 with named human faceless creators demonetized
   as collateral; TikTok RPMs crashed after the Jan 2026 US ownership transition and
   its originality filter targets exactly the faceless slideshow format; Instagram has
   no open payout program; Amazon Merch cut base royalties ~58% (June 2026) unless you
   bring your own external traffic.

Refuted as fabricated (0-3 verification votes — do not trust these when you see them
online): "3% of faceless channels succeed," "60–80% monetize by month 7–10," niche CPM
tables from faceless-tool vendors, "average Chrome extension earns $862k/yr," and every
"average Etsy seller makes $X" statistic.

## Why the ranking looks like this

**Recurring beats re-selling.** $2k/month *consistent* means either ~300 subscribers at
$7/mo (or ~130 B2B seats at $15/mo) that renew by default — or re-selling ~400 digital
downloads every single month forever. Subscriptions have memory; downloads don't. This
is the structural reason the dev paths outrank the content paths for a developer.

**Shopify apps (#1)**: 0% platform take on the first $1M lifetime (post-2025 terms),
native subscription billing, B2B customers who pay to solve revenue problems, $19 to
enter. Documented solo comps: $0→$1k MRR in 4 months (Mališauskas), $1k MRR at 7 months
(ABConvert), WideBundles $0→$50k MRR. The counterweight: a ~700-app cohort study showed
median first-3-months revenue of $0 — winners grind merchant outreach and reviews.

**Chrome extensions (#2)**: no marketplace tax at all (CWS payments died in 2021; you
bring Stripe or ExtensionPay at ~5%), huge distribution surface, real comps (Easy
Folders $3.7k MRR six months post-launch; GMass ~$130k MRR mature). Needs ~10–20k
active users at industry-typical 2% free→paid conversion for $2k.

**Digital catalogs (#3)** — the lane we already built. Verified: 80–95% margins; the
$250k comp came from moving up to $20 products; catalog size is the strongest earnings
predictor everywhere (Etsy, KDP: 25+ books → median $3k/mo, 1–3 books → <$100/mo).
Notion Marketplace (8% + $0.40) is the healthiest open template channel; Canva and
Figma paid-file programs are closed to new sellers.

**Faceless content (#9/#11)** is the inversion of the social-media pitch: it's the
highest-variance, most-platform-dependent, most actively-enforced-against option. The
2026 evidence says use content as **distribution for products you own** (posts driving
to your store/app), not as the revenue source itself. That flips platform risk from
"my income vanished" to "one marketing channel got quieter."

## The recommended stack (probability-weighted, ~10–15 hrs/wk)

Don't pick one — run a two-layer portfolio where every layer survives any single
platform's collapse:

1. **Core (recurring): one B2B micro-product.** Shopify app or Chrome extension
   solving one painful, searchable merchant/professional problem. This is the layer
   with a documented path to $2k+ MRR that *stays* $2k next month. Budget 8–15 months
   and expect the first idea to need a pivot.
2. **Compounding layer: the digital catalog you already started.** Keep stacking
   products (guides, template packs, printable collections, Notion templates) toward
   30–100 listings across Etsy/Ko-fi/Gumroad/Notion. Each item is small; the catalog
   compounds and cross-sells. Realistic contribution: $200–800/mo by month 6–12.
3. **Distribution, not monetization: faceless content.** Short-form posts showcasing
   the products/tool (original, transformative content — never slideshows/compilations)
   pointing at layers 1–2. $0 expected direct platform payout; its value is traffic
   you own the destination for.

Expected trajectory: months 1–3 ≈ $0–150/mo (catalog trickle); months 4–8 ≈ $300–900
(catalog + app's first paying users); months 9–15 ≈ break past $2k if the core product
finds fit — with the catalog as the floor if it doesn't. Failure mode to avoid: three
half-built products in month 4. Ship the catalog items weekly; give the core product
one focused bet at a time.
