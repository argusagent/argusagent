# Platform research — verified findings (July 2026)

Method: multi-angle web research (87 completed research agents), each claim below
survived 3-vote adversarial verification (independent agents tried to refute it
against the cited source). Votes shown as confirm-refute.

## Platform comparison

| Platform | Same-day live? | Upfront cost | Fees | Payout | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ko-fi** | ✅ Yes | $0 | 5% (free plan) + Stripe/PayPal processing | **Direct to your PayPal/Stripe, instant** | **PRIMARY** |
| **itch.io** | ✅ Yes (soft limits only) | $0 | You choose 0–100%, default 10% | "Direct to you" = instant; "Collected" = 7d + review (avoid) | SECONDARY |
| **Gumroad** | ✅ Yes | $0 | ~12.5% + $0.75 effective (10% + $0.50 advertised, on top of Stripe) | Weekly, platform holds funds | TERTIARY (risk) |
| **Etsy** | ❌ No | $15–29 nonrefundable setup fee | 6.5% transaction + $0.20/listing + processing | Delayed schedule for new shops | Skip for now |

## Key verified claims

### Etsy (all 3-0 confirmed)
- New-shop onboarding requires **photo ID upload + selfie** (verified via Persona). [Etsy Seller Handbook](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1241780194948)
- **$15–29 one-time nonrefundable setup fee** (varies by location), since Sept 2024. (same source)
- New shops face **modified/slower payout schedules** until they build a sales track record. (same source)

### Ko-fi (3-0 confirmed)
- Free plan: **no monthly fee, no listing fee, 5% service fee** on shop sales; Gold ($12/mo) removes the 5%. Stripe/PayPal processing (~2.9% + $0.30) applies on both. [creatorstackclub.com](https://www.creatorstackclub.com/software/ko-fi-shop)
- Free tier is **permanent** (not a trial): 25GB storage, up to 600 shop items. (same source)
- Supports digital downloads (PDFs, templates, presets, ebooks) with **instant delivery** and **pay-what-you-want** pricing. (same source)

### itch.io (3-0 confirmed)
- **Open revenue sharing**: seller sets itch.io's cut anywhere from 0–100%; default 10%. [itch.io FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq)
- Two payout modes: **"Direct to you"** (each sale goes straight to your PayPal/Stripe, money arrives immediately) vs "Collected by itch.io" (7-day hold, $5 minimum, 10–14 day review). (same source)
- ⚠️ Verified forum thread: a payout in "collected" mode stuck **"in review" for over 100 days**. [itch.io forum](https://itch.io/t/4400512/payout-stuck-in-review-for-over-100-days) → **always use "Direct to you."**
- New accounts face only soft anti-abuse limits (20 project pages), no approval gate. [itch.io FAQ]

### Gumroad (3-0 confirmed)
- Proven demand comp: **"Master Git in Minutes"**, a plain 27-page Git-commands PDF, did **105 sales / $734**; the live listing (devrimozcay.gumroad.com/l/gmmuu) independently corroborates. [Medium income breakdown](https://medium.com/write-a-catalyst/my-gumroad-income-breakdown-2-539-in-revenue-heres-what-worked-c0797855df76)
- ⚠️ Advertised 10% + $0.50 fee is **on top of Stripe fees** → effective ≈ **12.5% + $0.75**. [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/gumroad.com)
- ⚠️ Multiple verified reports of **account suspensions without warning and frozen payouts** on fully verified accounts. (same source)

## What this means for strategy

1. **Ko-fi first** — zero friction, money flows directly to your own PayPal/Stripe, worst case you lose nothing.
2. **itch.io second** with "Direct to you" payouts — the tools/assets audience there buys dev resources.
3. **Gumroad last, eyes open** — best organic discovery for dev PDFs, but never your only channel and never let a balance sit.
4. **Product-market fit signal:** simple, useful, no-fancy-design dev PDF guides demonstrably sell. Ship useful > ship pretty.
5. **Etsy later, if ever** — the fee + ID + payout friction only makes sense once products are proven elsewhere.

*Note: 17 of 104 verification agents failed on rate limits mid-run; every claim above completed its full 3-vote verification. Claims that didn't finish verification (e.g., secondary Gumroad Trustpilot color, Ko-fi e-commerce depth comparisons) were excluded from this document.*
